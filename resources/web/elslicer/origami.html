Origami animal

Prints flat as thin sheets with the fold lines grooved in. Fold it up by hand and glue the numbered tabs.

Sheet and fold lines

  1. Print as it lies, with no supports. The side on the plate becomes the outside.
  2. Fold along every line. Most fold with the grooves inside; the picture above shows the shape to aim for.
  3. Glue each tab under the edge with the same number, on the inside. Gel superglue or hot glue works.
  4. Fold each part, then glue the parts on where the picture shows them:
    • Thread elastic cord through the two holes at the sides.
