Back and stand

Drag the keyholes and the cable's way out in the Front view. Put them back

The stand is its own print: a foot with a slot as long as the box's bottom edge and as deep as the box. The box's lowest 6 mm push down into the slot and it stands up. It needs a flat stretch along the bottom; for a shape with a pointed bottom, hang it instead.
