Folding box

Prints flat as one thin sheet with the fold lines grooved in. Fold it up by hand: it locks shut without glue or tape.

Sheet and fold lines

Full sheetFold lines, thinYour plate
  1. Print it as it lies, with no supports. A textured plate and a slower first layer help the thin fold lines.
  2. Fold every line with the grooves on the inside: the floor's walls up, then the side walls' corner ears in.
  3. Roll each end over the ears and push its two tabs down through the slots in the floor.
