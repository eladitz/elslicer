What happens when you send

  1. Elslicer slices the plate with the new settings and shows the usual send window.
  2. It keeps only the layers above the part, lined up with its top.
  3. It cancels the paused print, puts the heaters straight back to the print's temperatures and tells the printer the bed is clear. The part stays on the bed.
  4. It sends the rest. The printer heats, lifts, homes X and Y only, primes in a corner and carries on. Bed levelling is switched off for this job.
Not proven on this printer yet. If the printer homes Z or levels by itself when the job starts, the nozzle could hit the part. Watch the start and stop it on the printer's screen if the nozzle heads down to the bed. If the printer has forgotten its height, it refuses to move instead.
